ARBITRATION AGREEMENT

UNDER SECTION 7 OF ARBITRATION & CONCILIATION ACT, 1996

THIS ARBITRAL SUBMISSION AGREEMENT is entered into on this 14th day of January, 2023, BETWEEN:

1. ABC PVT. LTD., a corporation incorporated under the laws of India, having its corporate seat at Nariman Point, Mumbai (Hereinafter referred to as the "Claimant");
